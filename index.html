04 / HOW TO READ THIS

Transparent about
the assumptions.

A good statistical summary is as clear about its limits as it is about its results.

From questionnaire to summary

The study uses a descriptive survey of 15 selected student records. The question analysed was: “How many hours do you spend on screens daily?” Respondents selected one of four bands: 2–3, 3–4, 4–5, or more than 5 hours.

For the estimated mean, the first three class midpoints are 2.5, 3.5, and 4.5 hours. The open-ended “More than 5 hours” group is assigned 5.5 hours solely for illustration, as if it were a 5–6 hour band.

(3 × 2.5) + (4 × 3.5) + (4 × 4.5) + (4 × 5.5) = 61.5

What the data cannot say

The exact mean and exact median time are unknown. The questionnaire records ranges only, and the final range has no upper limit. The 4.10-hour mean is an estimate based on an explicit midpoint assumption, not a directly measured average.

A CONCLUSION, WITH CONTEXT

Most responses sit at three hours or above. The exact average remains an estimate.

In this selected group, three categories tie for the highest frequency and the median falls in the 4–5 hour band. The data offers a useful snapshot, not a complete picture of student screen habits.
